Before starting inventory, check that the reader is ready:

1. Confirm that you can sign in to the reader web console.
2. Check that the regulatory region matches the reader's location and that the date and time are correct.
3. Check the reader dashboard or status information for errors or alerts that require attention.
4. Confirm that the antenna setup matches your installation and that the required antenna ports are configured for reading.
5. If an endpoint is already configured, check its connection status. An endpoint showing **connected** confirms the connection; it does not by itself confirm that the application receives messages.

For hardware indicators and model-specific checks, refer to the reader's integration guide. To configure MQTT and test tag delivery, continue to [Quickstart: Read a Tag and Send Data through MQTT](./quickstart-first-tag-to-mqtt-in-15-minutes.mdx).
