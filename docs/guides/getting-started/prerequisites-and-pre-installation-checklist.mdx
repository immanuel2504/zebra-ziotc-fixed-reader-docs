Before configuring the reader, prepare the equipment and network access required for your installation.

Use the [integration guide](https://www.zebra.com/content/dam/support-dam/en/documentation/unrestricted/guide/product/fxseries-ig-en.pdf) for hardware requirements and instructions to mount and power the reader, connect antennas and the network, and discover its address. For other reader models, find the applicable guide on [Zebra RFID Support](https://www.zebra.com/us/en/support-downloads/rfid.html).

After installation, obtain the reader's hostname or IP address and the credentials needed to sign in. Then continue to [Access the Reader Web Console](./discover-access-and-sign-in-to-the-reader.mdx).
