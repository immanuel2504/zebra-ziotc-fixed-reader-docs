Zebra IoT Connector (ZIoTC) connects Zebra fixed readers to applications that configure and control reader activity and receive reader data and events.

This section introduces the connector, the components of a reader solution, and the four interfaces: management, control, data, and management events. Review the deployment models and system requirements to plan your installation, then choose the topics that match your role.

When you are ready to install and configure the reader, continue to [Getting Started](../getting-started/index.mdx).
